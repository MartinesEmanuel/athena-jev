# METIS Calibration

2026-09-18 calibration used 20 loop and 20 progress cases with real TypeSafe Jev. It is a small, constructed dataset, not a general benchmark.

Production questions separate strategy similarity, unresolved problem, superficial variation, new information, expected progress, and change recommendation. State includes compact action results, deterministic strategy families, and repeated error families.

Policy uses a transparent composite score. It also requires low new information and four independent stagnation signals. Threshold sweep had no progress false positives from 0.50 through 0.85. `0.75` was selected to retain margin over lower candidates while covering observed real-session score near 0.76. Shadow remains non-intervening. Existing scheduling and cooldown prevent repeated immediate recommendations.

Raw scores, question variants, threshold sweep, and final confusion breakdown are stored under `evals/metis-calibration/`.
